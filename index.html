Camera access

ScanSpec needs your camera

The camera is how the app reads the QR label printed on a Northcomm product. That is the only thing it is used for.

  • The camera only turns on while you are on the scan screen.
  • Label images are read on your phone and are never uploaded or saved.
  • No photos or video are ever recorded.
