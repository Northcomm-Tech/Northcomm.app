Northcomm ScanSpec

Northcomm ScanSpec pulls up the factory test report for a cable assembly. Scan the QR label on the assembly, and its report opens.

Need help?
Email support@northcomm.app

Scanning a label

  1. Tap Scan a QR label and allow camera access the first time.
  2. Hold the label inside the frame. It reads automatically, no button to press.
  3. Use the zoom slider if the label is small, or the flashlight in poor light.

Nothing comes up after a scan

If the app says no report is on file, the scan worked but that serial has no document uploaded yet. The screen shows the exact serial it read. Contact whoever maintains the report library and give them that serial.

The report will not open

The report opens on the result screen. Tap Save PDF to keep a copy on your device. Reports are large documents, so on a slow connection one can take a moment to arrive. If the screen still looks empty, tap Scan another and try the label again.

Typing a serial instead of scanning

On the home screen choose Enter a part number manually and type the serial as printed on the label.

Adding the app to your home screen

Install Northcomm ScanSpec from the App Store. There is also a browser version at northcomm-tech.github.io/Northcomm.app if you would rather not install anything.

Accounts

Northcomm ScanSpec requires an account. Sign in with your email address and password, or tap Create an account on the first screen. Your account keeps your recent scans in sync across your devices. To remove it, open Settings from the top of the screen and choose Delete my account. That removes your sign-in and permanently unlinks your scan history from you. Test reports for Northcomm assemblies stay on file, because they belong to the hardware rather than to your account.
