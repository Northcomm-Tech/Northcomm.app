Northcomm Specs

Northcomm Specs pulls up the factory test report for a cable assembly. Scan the QR label on the assembly, and its report opens.

Need help?
Email support@northcomm.app

Scanning a label

  1. Tap Scan a QR label and allow camera access the first time.
  2. Hold the label inside the frame. It reads automatically, no button to press.
  3. Use the zoom slider if the label is small, or the flashlight in poor light.

Nothing comes up after a scan

If the app says no report is on file, the scan worked but that serial has no document uploaded yet. The screen shows the exact serial it read. Contact whoever maintains the report library and give them that serial.

The report will not open

Reports are large documents and can take a moment on a slow connection. If nothing happens automatically, tap View full report at the top of the card. Download PDF saves a copy to your device.

Typing a serial instead of scanning

On the home screen choose Enter a part number manually and type the serial as printed on the label.

Adding the app to your home screen

iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: open the app in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.

Accounts

An account is optional and only syncs your recent scans between devices. Scanning and viewing reports works without one. To remove your account, open the home screen and choose Delete my account.
