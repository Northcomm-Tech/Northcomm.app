Questions

Good to know.

Where do I find the serial number?
It's printed on the label attached to the cable assembly, usually beginning with "NC" followed by a number — for example NC-121483. Some labels also carry a QR code that opens the report directly.
My serial isn't found. What now?
Double-check the digits against the label. If it still isn't found, the report may not be published yet. Reach out and Northcomm will confirm the assembly and upload its report.
What's in a report?
Each report is the factory sweep test for that exact assembly — VSWR and insertion loss across the swept frequency range, the pass/fail result, and the test date. The full PDF is the document that shipped with the cable.
Do I need an account?
No. Anyone with a serial number can look up its published report here in the browser. No sign-in required.
Can't find a report?

If a serial isn't on file yet, get in touch and Northcomm will track down the assembly and publish its test report.
