Privacy Policy

Northcomm ScanSpec · last updated 6 September 2026

Northcomm ScanSpec is an internal tool published by North Comm Technologies. It lets staff scan the QR label on a product and pull up that assembly's factory test report.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password. An account is required to use the app. Your email address is used only to sign you in, and your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash that nobody at North Comm can read.
  • Your scan history — the serial numbers you have looked up and when. This lets the app show your recent scans across your devices.

That is the entire list. We do not collect your name, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your camera

The app uses your camera only while the scanner screen is open, and only to read QR codes. Images from the camera are processed on your device as the scanner looks for a code. No photo or video is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

How your data is stored

Account details and scan history are held in North Comm Technologies' own database, hosted by Supabase. Access rules restrict each account to reading only its own scan history. Test report documents themselves contain no personal information.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or data brokers. The only third party that handles your data is Supabase, which hosts our database on our behalf and processes it solely under our instructions. Your data is otherwise disclosed only if we are required to do so by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and your scan history for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, your sign-in is removed immediately and your scan history is permanently unlinked from you, so it can never be traced back to you again.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open Settings from the top of the screen and choose Delete my account. Your sign-in is removed immediately and permanently, and your scan history is permanently unlinked from you so that it can never be traced back to you again. Test reports for Northcomm assemblies stay on file, because they describe the hardware rather than you. You may also email the address below and we will do it for you.

Children

This is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests to access or delete your data: info@northcommtechnologies.com
