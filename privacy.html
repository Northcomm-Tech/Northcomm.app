Privacy Policy

Northcomm Specs · last updated 22 August 2026

Northcomm Specs is an internal tool published by Northcomm Technologies. It lets staff scan the QR label on a cable assembly and pull up that assembly's factory test report.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you create an account. This is used only to sign you in.
  • Your scan history — the serial numbers you have looked up and when. This lets the app show your recent scans across your devices.

That is the entire list. We do not collect your name, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your camera

The app uses your camera only while the scanner screen is open, and only to read QR codes. Images from the camera are processed on your device as the scanner looks for a code. No photo or video is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

How your data is stored

Account details and scan history are held in Northcomm Technologies' own database, hosted by Supabase. Access rules restrict each account to reading only its own scan history. Test report documents themselves contain no personal information.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. We do not use advertising networks. Data is disclosed only if required by law.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the home screen and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your login and your scan history. You may also email the address below and we will do it for you.

Children

This is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests to access or delete your data: support@northcomm.app
